Privacy Policy — Brand Uploader

Last updated: October 4, 2026

What this app does

Brand Uploader is a private tool used by its operator to upload videos to the operator's own YouTube channels via the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Data we access

Through Google OAuth, the app accesses:

  • YouTube channel information for channels the operator manages (to verify the correct upload destination)
  • The ability to upload videos and set video metadata (titles, descriptions, thumbnails) on those channels

OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens are stored securely and are never logged, displayed, or written to unprotected files.

How data is used

Accessed data is used solely to publish the operator's videos to the operator's YouTube channels. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Data retention

OAuth tokens are retained only as long as the automation is in use and are revoked on request. Uploaded video content remains on YouTube under the operator's channels per YouTube's own terms.
