Brand Uploader

Brand Uploader is a private automation tool that uploads short-form videos to the owner's own YouTube channels using the YouTube Data API.

It is not a public service and does not accept sign-ups. It exists solely to publish content to channels owned and managed by the operator.

What it does:

  • Uploads video files to the operator's YouTube channels
  • Sets titles, descriptions, and thumbnails on those uploads
  • Marks AI-generated content using YouTube's synthetic media disclosure
